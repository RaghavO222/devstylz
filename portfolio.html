CAD-Vision-MCP — AI Vision & Geometric CAD Engine

A high-precision Model Context Protocol (MCP) server bridging Large Language Models (Anthropic Claude Vision) with raw DXF drawings to solve automated telecom tower antenna clash detection and geometric compliance.

Geometric Spatial Reconstruction: Overcame unorganized "exploded" CAD drawings with thousands of disconnected lines by clustering geometry into logical antenna entities using spatial proximity.
Dual AI & Math Architecture: Claude Vision matches DXF labels to physical antenna fixtures in steelwork photography via azimuth cues, while Shapely/NumPy computes exact 3D radiation cones.
Automated Clash Detection: Mathematically slides candidate antennas along structural rails of the headframe to calculate clearance from existing equipment and locate 100% clash-free coordinates.
